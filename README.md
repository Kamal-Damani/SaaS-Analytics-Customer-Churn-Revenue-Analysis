# 📊 SaaS Business Analytics — Customer, Subscription & Revenue Analysis

## 🔎 Project Overview

An end-to-end **SaaS Business Analytics project** using **MySQL and PowerPoint** to analyze customer behavior, subscriptions, churn, acquisition costs, and revenue performance.

The project transforms raw business data into actionable insights through **SQL analysis, Excel , and business storytelling**.

### Key Areas Covered

* Customer acquisition and growth
* Active vs. churned customers
* Churn by subscription plan
* Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
* Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
* Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
* Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
* CAC Payback Period
* Cohort revenue analysis
* Customer revenue ranking
* Monthly and cumulative revenue

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to evaluate SaaS business performance by analyzing **customers, subscriptions, and revenue**.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Structure

The project uses three normalized MySQL tables:

* **Customers** — Customer details, signup dates, and churn status
* **Subscriptions** — Subscription plans and billing information
* **Revenue** — Revenue records and transaction details

The tables are connected using **SQL JOINs** to analyze customer, subscription, and revenue performance.
